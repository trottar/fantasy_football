# v1.X Prospective Season Development Context

This record defines stable scientific rules for the empirical 2026 development
series. It is not the active-state document.

## 0.X / 1.X boundary

`0.X` is the a-priori architecture frozen before observed 2026 outcomes.

`1.X` is the prospective Data/MC closure and evidence-supported evolution
series.

Observed 2026 outcomes may:

- open investigations;
- identify data defects;
- reveal structural representation gaps;
- diagnose uncertainty;
- support v1.X calibration after adequate evidence.

They may not retroactively turn a tuned model into an a-priori `0.X` model.

## Physics decomposition

Preserve:

`N_fantasy ~ L x sigma x A x epsilon`

and:

`P ⊕ D ⊕ K`

Players compare to players, DST to DST, kickers to kickers. Cross-channel
coupling belongs at full-roster utility/state boundaries.

Manager behavior is a separate stochastic response channel from football
utility.

## Prospective evidence levels

### Week-open reference

Provides a stable comparable weekly experimental reference.

### Decision-time capture

Provides the exact information state used for a consequential action as later
news legitimately arrives.

Both require:

`information_time <= decision_time < relevant_outcome_time`

A missed capture is recorded as missing.

## Closure sequence

For prediction `M_i`, observation `D_i`, uncertainty `sigma_i`:

`r_i = D_i - M_i`

`z_i = (D_i - M_i) / sigma_i`

Evaluate:

- residual distributions;
- MAE;
- RMSE;
- pull mean/width;
- interval coverage;
- availability Brier scores;
- channel-specific components;
- regret;
- behavior-model probabilities.

Raw observations remain immutable evidence. Derived/calibrated state remains
separate.

## Calendar gates versus evidence gates

Calendar gates protect prospective measurement/operations.

Evidence gates authorize scientific change.

If a calendar review arrives with inadequate evidence, the model remains
unchanged and the gate result is `DEFER / COLLECT MORE DATA`.

## Calibration policy

### One game

May open an investigation.

Does not authorize broad tuning.

### Repeated local discrepancy

Diagnose:

- input;
- availability;
- role/opportunity;
- efficiency;
- matchup;
- structural representation;
- uncertainty.

### Repeated population/channel discrepancy

May become a calibration candidate.

### Production authorization

Requires prospective evidence and validation showing the candidate improves the
relevant closure without degrading uncertainty, causality, or subsystem
boundaries.

## Playoff freeze

Before Week 14, commission a playoff production baseline.

Weeks 14-17 are production-first:

- no opportunistic broad retuning;
- no response to one playoff outcome by changing empirical parameters;
- structural correctness defects may be repaired when the architecture cannot
  represent the real process;
- playoff outcomes continue to enter immutable closure and normally inform
  postseason research.

## Season success

Scientific success is not identical to one fantasy-season result.

A successful season preserves:

- causal decisions;
- frozen prospective state;
- uncertainty;
- channel separation;
- behavior/physics separation;
- weekly closure;
- diagnosable failures;
- evidence-supported changes;
- negative results;
- reproducible provenance.
