# Player Channel

Scope: QB/RB/WR/TE.

The player channel is the mature football-production channel and should remain physically interpretable.

Core decomposition:

`opportunity -> production efficiency -> matchup acceptance -> fantasy scoring response`

Availability and workload are explicit stochastic components.

Player comparisons are player-to-player only. D/ST and K do not enter player ranking/trade/waiver comparisons as peer assets.

Defensive/matchup information may influence player output as higher-order conditioning without violating sector separation.

A cheap deterministic screen may generate candidate actions but cannot authorize them. Predictive MC/paired response remains the authority.
